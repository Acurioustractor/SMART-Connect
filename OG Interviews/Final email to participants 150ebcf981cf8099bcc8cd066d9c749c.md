Dear SMART Storytellers,

We wanted to share with you the incredible impact your photos had at our recent SMART Stories celebration event! While not everyone could be there in person, your stories and perspectives filled the room in such a meaningful way.

Let us paint the picture for you: We displayed all of your amazing photos as magnets on fridges (yes, actual fridges!) and laid them out on tables for people to explore. Each image sparked conversations about recovery, hope, and the beauty found in everyday moments. The SMART Recovery community members who attended were deeply moved by your visual stories, and everyone got to take home photo magnets that resonated with them.

Even though you couldn't all be there physically, your presence was felt through every photo shared. Each image told a story that helped others feel less alone in their journey. For those who couldn't make it, we want you to know that your contribution made a real difference - your perspectives and experiences matter deeply to our community.

4. Share Your Ideas: Maybe you've thought of ways we could make this project even better? Or have ideas for other creative ways to share recovery stories? We're all ears!
