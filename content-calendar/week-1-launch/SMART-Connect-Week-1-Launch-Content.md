### Welcome to SMART Connect Community 👋

**This space exists for one reason: to support you as a facilitator.**

Too many facilitator communities become:
- ❌ One-way announcement boards
- ❌ Places where only confident people post
- ❌ Another thing to check that never has anything new

We're building something different:

**✅ Peer-to-peer:** Questions answered by facilitators, not just staff
**✅ Practical:** Real scenarios, real solutions, real resources
**✅ Respectful of time:** No guilt if you lurk. No pressure to perform.
**✅ Culturally safe:** Especially for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander facilitators

---

### How This Community Works

**📱 Jump in when you need it**
No requirement to visit daily. Check in when you have a question or want connection.

**💬 Every contribution matters**
- First-time comment? Gold.
- Answering from experience? Invaluable.
- Sharing a resource? Thank you.

**🔒 Safe to be real**
This is a closed community for verified facilitators. What's shared here stays here.

### Community Guidelines (The Short Version)

- **Be real.** Perfection isn't required or helpful.
- **Be respectful.** Diverse experiences make us stronger.
- **Assume good intent.** Text lacks tone.
- **Protect privacy.** No participant stories without de-identification.
- **Cultural safety is non-negotiable.** Especially for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander facilitators.

## 🖤❤️💛 Mob Yarning Space Welcome Post

**Posted by:** Cultural Safety Moderator (Aboriginal staff or Community Champion)
**Location:** Mob Yarning Space (Private Forum)
**Posted:** Week 1 (only visible to invited Aboriginal facilitators)

### Welcome to Mob Yarning Space 🖤❤️💛

**This is your space.**

**Who this is for:** Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander facilitators only.

**Why it's private:** Because cultural safety isn't negotiable, and sometimes you need to yarn with Mob without explaining yourself.

**What this space is for:**
- Sharing how you've adapted SMART for your community
- Yarning about challenges that non-Indigenous facilitators don't face
- Connecting with other Mob facilitators
- Asking questions without code-switching
- Celebrating what's working in your communities

**What you won't find here:**
- Tokenism
- Performative acknowledgements
- Pressure to educate others about Indigenous issues
- Your contributions used elsewhere without permission

---

### How This Space Works

**1. Your contributions stay here**
Nothing shared in Mob Yarning Space leaves this space without explicit permission.

**2. You control your visibility**
Want to share something with the broader SMART community? Your choice. Want to keep it here? That stays here.

**3. Cultural protocols are respected**
If something doesn't feel culturally safe, speak up. We'll address it.

**4. Your work is valued**
Adapting SMART for your community is skilled, important work. It's not "changing the program"—it's making it work.

## 🚨 Week 1 Troubleshooting Guide

### **Problem: Low Engagement (fewer than 10 people posting by Day 3)**

**Diagnosis:**
- Content not resonating?
- Founding Facilitators too busy this week?
- Platform friction (hard to access/navigate)?
- Topics too generic?

**Action:**
1. Send personal messages to 5-7 Founding Facilitators: "What would make this worth your time?"
2. Post a vulnerable, specific question from real facilitator (with permission)
3. Host optional 15-minute "Community Walkthrough" call
4. Reduce posting frequency to every other day

---

### **Problem: Only Staff Are Responding**

**Diagnosis:**
- Facilitators treating it like announcement board
- Questions too broad or hypothetical
- Staff responding too quickly (not leaving space for peers)

**Action:**
1. Staff wait 2-4 hours before responding to give peers chance to answer
2. When staff do respond, ask "Other facilitators: what's your experience?"
3. Post very specific, practical scenario questions
4. Privately message 3-5 experienced facilitators to ask for their input on specific threads

---

### **Problem: Technical Issues (login problems, access errors)**

**Diagnosis:**
- LearnWorlds configuration issues
- Invitation links not working
- Mobile access problems

**Action:**
1. Post acknowledgement in Welcome forum: "We're aware some folks are having login issues. Working on it."
2. Create alternative access method (direct email with temp password)
3. Set up office hours for tech support (Zoom drop-in)
4. Test on multiple devices/browsers

---

### **Problem: Inappropriate Post or Comment**

**Diagnosis:**
- Community Guidelines violated
- Participant privacy compromised
- Culturally unsafe content

**Action:**
1. Remove post/comment immediately if it violates safety
2. Private message to poster explaining why
3. If cultural safety issue: immediate check-in with Cultural Safety Moderator
4. If serious: remove person's access and consult with SMART leadership
5. Post general reminder of Guidelines without naming names

---

### **Problem: Someone Dominates Discussions**

**Diagnosis:**
- One person commenting on every thread
- Others may feel crowded out

**Action:**
1. Private message: "Thanks for being so engaged! To make sure others have space to contribute, would you mind holding back on a few threads to see who else jumps in?"
2. Directly invite quiet facilitators to specific threads
3. Post questions specifically seeking diverse voices: "Would love to hear from facilitators of [specific meeting type]"

---

### **Problem: Mob Yarning Space Has No Activity**

**Diagnosis:**
- Aboriginal facilitators testing safety first (normal and appropriate)
- Unclear purpose
- Moderation concerns

**Action:**
1. **Do not push.** Silence is data.
2. Cultural Safety Moderator posts personal story first to model vulnerability
3. One-on-one check-ins with Aboriginal facilitators: "Does this space feel safe? What would make it useful?"
4. Consider: maybe written forum isn't the right format. Would regular Yarning Circle calls work better?
5. Revisit protocols with Cultural Safety Moderator

## 📝 Notes for Community Manager

**Your job in Week 1 is NOT to:**
- ❌ Have all the answers
- ❌ Keep conversation going 24/7
- ❌ Make everyone participate
- ❌ Be perfect

**Your job in Week 1 IS to:**
- ✅ Respond quickly and warmly
- ✅ Model vulnerability and authenticity
- ✅ Make peer-to-peer exchanges visible and valued
- ✅ Create space for diverse voices
- ✅ Listen for what facilitators actually need
- ✅ Adjust quickly based on what you learn

**Remember:**
- **Silence is okay.** Not every thread needs 50 comments.
- **Lurking is okay.** Some people need to watch before they participate.
- **Slow is okay.** You're building trust, not manufacturing engagement.

**Your tone should be:**
- Warm but professional
- Authentic but boundaried
- Facilitative but not performative
- Responsive but not reactive

**When in doubt:**
- Ask facilitators what they need
- Err on the side of less content, more space
- Trust the process
- Remember: this is version 1.0 of many
