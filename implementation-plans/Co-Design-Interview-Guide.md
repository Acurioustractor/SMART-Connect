# SMART Connect Co-Design Interview Guide
## Testing Community Design with Facilitators

**Purpose:** Validate community structure, explore LearnWorlds functionality, co-create cultural safety protocols, and recruit Founding Facilitators

**Duration:** 90 minutes
**Honorarium:** $100 gift card
**Format:** Video call or in-person

---

## Interview Structure & Script

### Opening (10 minutes)

**Consent & Recording:**
"Thanks so much for being here. Before we start, I want to confirm a few things:
- Is it okay if I record this session? It's just for my notes - we'll transcribe key insights but your name won't be attached to specific quotes without your permission
- Everything you share will help shape the community, and you can be as honest as you want - there are no wrong answers
- You can opt out of any question or stop at any time
- We'll send you the transcript afterward so you can edit or remove anything

Do you have any questions before we start?"

[Get verbal consent, start recording]

**Context Setting:**
"Let me give you some quick context on where we're at:

We've interviewed 15+ facilitators like yourself and surveyed 219 facilitators about what would make an online community valuable. Here's what we learned:
- 78% want discussion boards to connect with peers
- 70% want regular news and updates
- 61% want a place to ask questions and get help
- 47% want help finding co-facilitators
- Only 9% want badges and gamification (so we're NOT doing that!)

The biggest thing we heard: facilitators are time-poor volunteers who feel isolated and want practical peer support that fits into your lives.

So we've designed a community structure based on that research, and today I want to show it to you, get your honest reactions, and make sure we're building something you'd actually use.

Sound good?"

### Part 1: First Impressions Walkthrough (30 minutes)

**Show Overview:**
"Let me show you what we're thinking. This is a mockup of the SMART Connect community in LearnWorlds..."

[Share screen showing forum structure]

**Questions:**

1. **First Reaction:**
   - "What's your first impression?"
   - "What stands out to you - good or bad?"
   - "Does this feel overwhelming, inviting, confusing, or something else?"

2. **Navigation:**
   - "If you wanted to ask other facilitators a question, where would you go?"
   - "If you wanted to find a resource like a session plan template, where would you look?"
   - "If you wanted to connect with other [LGBTQIA+/Family & Friends/Aboriginal] facilitators, where would you go?"
   - *[Note what's easy vs hard to find]*

3. **Mobile Experience:**
   - "Do you usually access things like this on your phone or computer?"
   - [If phone] "Let me show you what it looks like on mobile..."
   - "Would you use this on your phone?"
   - "What would make it easier to post from your phone?"
   - *[Note: voice-to-text? Image uploads? Simplified posting?]*

4. **The Spaces:**
   "We've created different spaces for different needs. Let me walk you through them..."

   **Yarning Circle (Main Discussion):**
   - "This is the main discussion area. Would you feel comfortable posting here?"
   - "What kind of things would you post or ask about?"
   - "What would stop you from posting?"

   **Communities of Practice:**
   - "We have spaces for specific groups - LGBTQIA+ facilitators, Family & Friends, Justice, Youth, and a private space for Aboriginal facilitators"
   - "Would you participate in [relevant CoP for them]?"
   - "What discussions would be valuable there?"
   - "How is this different from the main space?"

   **Resources & Tools:**
   - "This is where facilitators can share templates, guides, scripts"
   - "Would you upload something here? What kind of thing?"
   - "Would you download others' stuff?"
   - "What would make it easier to share resources?"

   **Connection Hub:**
   - "This is for finding co-facilitators or coverage"
   - "Would you use this? How?"
   - "What else would help facilitators connect?"

5. **What's Missing:**
   - "Is there anything you'd expect to see that's not here?"
   - "What would make this more useful for you?"

---

### Part 2: Feature Deep Dive (20 minutes)

**Show Specific Features:**

**1. Discussion Thread Example**

[Show mockup of a discussion thread with posts/replies]

"This is what a discussion would look like. Someone posts a question, others reply..."

**Questions:**
- "Would you feel comfortable posting a question like this?"
- "Would you reply to someone else's question?"
- "What would make you more likely to respond to others?"
- "How do you feel about notifications? Would you want to know when someone replies? Or would that be annoying?"
- "Should people be able to reply anonymously, or should we see who's posting?"
  - *[Research says facilitators want accountability, not anonymity - but check this]*

**2. Resource Upload**

[Show how someone would upload a file/template]

"This is how you'd share a resource - a session plan, a script, a tool adaptation..."

**Questions:**
- "Does this look easy or complicated?"
- "What format would you share? Word doc? PDF? Handwritten photo?"
- "What would you need to know before uploading? (Privacy? Copyright?)"
- "Would you want feedback on resources you share? Likes? Comments?"

**3. Email Digest**

[Show mockup of bi-weekly email]

"We're thinking of sending a digest every 2 weeks with highlights - top discussions, new resources, upcoming events..."

**Questions:**
- "Would you read this?"
- "Is every 2 weeks the right frequency? Too much? Too little?"
- "What should be in here that would make you actually click through to the community?"
- "Should you be able to reply directly to the email and have it post to the thread?"

**4. Facilitator Spotlight**

[Show example of facilitator being featured]

"We want to feature different facilitators each week - sharing their story, approach, what they've learned..."

**Questions:**
- "Would you be willing to be featured?"
- "What would you want to share? What would you NOT want to share?"
- "How long should this be? (Video? Written? Audio?)"
- "What would make this feel authentic vs performative or braggy?"
- *[Note cultural concerns about being "big-noted"]*

**5. Direct Messaging (If Available in LearnWorlds)**

"Facilitators could message each other directly..."

**Questions:**
- "Would you use this? For what?"
- "Or would you rather keep conversations public in discussion threads?"
- "What boundaries would you want? (e.g., not available 24/7)"

### Part 3: Cultural Safety Deep Dive (20 minutes)

**[ESPECIALLY IMPORTANT WITH ABORIGINAL FACILITATOR - THIS CO-DESIGNS MOB SPACE]**

**For Aboriginal Facilitator Specifically:**

"One of the clearest things we heard from Aboriginal facilitators is that cultural safety is non-negotiable. We want to create a private 'Mob Yarning Space' specifically for Aboriginal facilitators. But we need to do this right, with your guidance."

[Show proposed Mob space structure]

**Questions:**

1. **Space Design:**
   - "Does this feel safe to you?"
   - "What protocols need to be in place?"
   - "Should this be completely private? Or visible but invite-only?"
   - "How should invitations work? Who can invite?"

2. **What Belongs Here:**
   - "What would you want to discuss in this space that you wouldn't discuss publicly?"
   - "Cultural adaptation of SMART tools?"
   - "Challenges working in community?"
   - "Connection with other mob facilitators?"
   - "What else?"

3. **Moderation & Governance:**
   - "Who should moderate this space?"
   - "What training do they need?"
   - "How should conflicts be handled?"
   - "Who makes decisions about the space?"
   - "Should there be an advisory group? Who should be on it?"

4. **Ground Rules:**
   - "What ground rules would make you feel safe here?"
   - "What language/behaviors are not okay?"
   - "How do we honor different Aboriginal communities' protocols?"
   - "What happens if someone shares something outside the space without consent?"

5. **Recognition:**
   - "Research shows facilitators are cautious about badges and gamification"
   - "Some Aboriginal facilitators mentioned concerns about being 'big-noted'"
   - "How should we recognize contributions in culturally appropriate ways?"
   - "What does good recognition look like in your community?"

6. **Reversible Consent:**
   - "Should you be able to edit or delete your posts anytime?"
   - "What if someone else already replied to it?"
   - "How do we balance transparency with cultural safety?"

**For ALL Facilitators:**

**Broader Cultural Safety Questions:**

1. **What Could Go Wrong:**
   - "What are you worried about in a community like this?"
   - "What kinds of conflicts might arise?"
   - "What would make you feel unsafe or uncomfortable?"

2. **Ground Rules:**
   - "What ground rules do we need?"
   - "Research suggests 'group agreements' rather than 'ground rules' - thoughts?"
   - "What language/behaviors should not be tolerated?"
   - "How do we handle mistakes vs intentional harm?"

3. **Moderation:**
   - "What should moderators do?"
   - "What should they NOT do?"
   - "Should moderation be visible (public warnings) or private?"
   - "How would you want to be approached if you posted something problematic?"

4. **Reporting:**
   - "If you saw something concerning, what would you do?"
   - "Should there be a 'report post' button?"
   - "Who should receive reports?"
   - "How quickly should they be addressed?"

5. **Diverse Facilit

ators:**
   - "How do we make sure all kinds of facilitators feel welcome?"
   - "What would make this feel inclusive vs exclusionary?"
   - "Are there other identity-based spaces we should create?"

---

### Part 4: Content Strategy Feedback (10 minutes)

**Show Content Calendar:**

"We're thinking about a weekly rhythm for content:
- **Monday:** Inspiration & Connection (facilitator spotlight, wins, news)
- **Wednesday:** Peer Support (check-in thread, Q&A)
- **Friday:** Resources & Tools (template shares, practical tips)"

**Questions:**

1. **Rhythm:**
   - "Is this too much? Too little? Right amount?"
   - "What day of the week are you most likely to check in?"
   - "Should weekends be quiet or is that when you have time?"

2. **Content Types:**
   "What content would bring you back weekly?"
   - Facilitator stories?
   - Practical resources?
   - Discussion threads where you can ask questions?
   - News about SMART policy changes?
   - Participant impact stories?
   - Something else?

3. **Who Creates Content:**
   - "Right now, most content would come from staff to get things started"
   - "How soon should facilitators be leading discussions?"
   - "Would you be willing to start a discussion thread? On what topic?"
   - "What would make you feel confident to post, not just comment?"

4. **Email Digest:**
   - "We talked about bi-weekly email - let me show you a mockup..."
   [Show example]
   - "Would this make you click through to the community?"
   - "What's the hook that would get you to read it?"
   - "Subject line: 'What You Missed in SMART Connect' - compelling or annoying?"

---

### Part 5: Barriers & Motivators (10 minutes)

**Barriers Deep Dive:**

"Research tells us the #1 barrier is time. But let's dig into what would stop YOU specifically..."

1. **Time:**
   - "How much time per week could you realistically spend in the community?"
   - "5 minutes? 15 minutes? 30 minutes?"
   - "When would you check in? Morning? Lunch? Evening? Weekend?"
   - "What would make it fast enough to be worth it?"

2. **Confidence:**
   - "Would you worry about saying the wrong thing?"
   - "What would make you feel more confident to post?"
   - "Should there be a 'welcome to new posters' vibe?"

3. **Tech Barriers:**
   - "Is the platform itself easy enough to use?"
   - "Do you need a tutorial? Or should it be intuitive?"
   - "Mobile or computer - what's easier for you?"

4. **Relevance:**
   - "How do you know if the community has stuff relevant to you?"
   - "Should you be able to filter by topic/demographic?"
   - "Would tags help? (e.g., #youth #justice #culturalSafety)"

**Motivators Deep Dive:**

"What would make you come back weekly?"

1. **Connection:**
   - "Meeting other facilitators like you?"
   - "Finding a co-facilitator?"
   - "Feeling less alone in this work?"

2. **Practical Value:**
   - "Getting answers to questions faster?"
   - "Finding resources you can use immediately?"
   - "Learning new approaches from peers?"

3. **Recognition:**
   - "Being thanked for helping others?"
   - "Being featured as a spotlight?"
   - "Having your resources used by others?"
   - *[But remember: only 9% want badges!]*

4. **Belonging:**
   - "Feeling part of something bigger?"
   - "Knowing you're contributing to SMART's mission?"
   - "Being in community with people who get it?"

**The Deciding Factor:**

"If you could change ONE thing to make this community irresistible to you, what would it be?"

*[This is gold - write it down verbatim]*

### Part 6: The Big Asks (5 minutes)

**1. Founding Facilitator Invitation:**

"We want to launch this with 20-30 'Founding Facilitators' who will:
- Join in the first week
- Post at least once per week for the first month
- Invite 2-3 other facilitators to join
- Give us honest feedback on what's working and what's not

**Would you be a Founding Facilitator?**"

*[If yes: Get commitment, add to list]*
*[If hesitant: "What would you need to say yes?"]*

**2. Community Champion Invitation:**

"We're also recruiting 5-10 'Community Champions' who will:
- Be active in specific spaces (like LGBTQIA+ or Family & Friends CoP)
- Welcome new members
- Seed discussions and respond to questions
- Help with moderation (light touch, supportive)
- Spend 3-5 hours per month
- Receive $50-100/month stipend OR meaningful recognition

**Would you consider being a Community Champion?**"

*[If yes: Discuss which space, expectations, stipend vs recognition]*
*[If no: "What would make this role more appealing?"]*

**3. One Last Thing:**

"If you could give us just ONE piece of advice - one thing we absolutely must get right - what would it be?"

*[This is the North Star quote - capture it exactly]*

## Appendix: Question Bank

**If you have extra time or want to go deeper, here are additional questions:**

### On Peer Support Patterns:

- "When you've gotten stuck as a facilitator, who did you turn to?"
- "Have you ever helped another facilitator? What did that look like?"
- "What would make you feel confident giving advice to other facilitators?"
- "Do you learn more from facilitators similar to you or different from you?"

### On Community Examples:

- "Are you part of any other online communities? (professional or personal)"
- "What makes you come back to those communities?"
- "What annoys you about online communities you've been in?"
- "Have you seen any communities do something really well that we should copy?"

### On Content Preferences:

- "Video, audio, or text - what's easiest for you?"
- "Do you prefer long, detailed posts or short, quick ones?"
- "How do you feel about memes/humor in professional communities?"
- "Should content be polished/professional or casual/authentic?"

### On Recognition:

- "How do you like to be thanked for helping others?"
- "Public recognition or private thank-you?"
- "What would feel meaningful vs performative?"
- "In your culture/community, how are contributions usually recognized?"

### On Connection:

- "Would you want to know more about other facilitators' backgrounds?"
- "Profiles with photos and bios? Or keep it more anonymous?"
- "Would you attend an in-person facilitator gathering if it was local?"
- "How do you build trust with other facilitators online?"

## Success Metrics for Co-Design Process

**You'll know the interviews were successful if:**

✅ 5-7 diverse facilitators participated
✅ You have clear, actionable feedback on community structure
✅ Mob Yarning Space protocols co-created with Aboriginal facilitator(s)
✅ At least 15 Founding Facilitators recruited
✅ At least 3 Community Champions recruited
✅ You've identified 3-5 "must change" items before launch
✅ You feel confident the design will work for real facilitators
✅ Facilitators are excited and committed to joining

**Red Flags:**
⚠️ Facilitators seem confused by structure
⚠️ Aboriginal facilitator doesn't feel Mob space is safe yet
⚠️ No one wants to be a Founding Facilitator (design not compelling)
⚠️ Every facilitator mentions the same barrier
⚠️ Time commitment feels unrealistic even to engaged facilitators

---

## Final Note

**Remember:** These facilitators are giving you their time and wisdom. Honor that by:
- Actually listening (not just waiting to present your ideas)
- Being open to major changes based on their feedback
- Following up on what you promised
- Showing how their input shaped the final design
- Inviting them to be part of the launch and success

**They are not testers. They are co-designers. This is their community as much as yours.**

Good luck! 🌱
