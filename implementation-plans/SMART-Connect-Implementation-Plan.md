# SMART Connect Implementation Plan
## Building a Thriving Facilitator Community in LearnWorlds

**Version:** 1.0
**Date:** November 2025
**Grounded In:** Research from 15+ interviews, 219 survey responses, world-class community patterns

---

## Executive Summary

This plan transforms research insights into actionable community design for SMART Connect using LearnWorlds forums. It includes:
- Community architecture (spaces, channels, rhythms)
- 12-month engagement roadmap
- Content strategy and schedules
- Moderation framework
- Success metrics
- Co-design testing approach with 5+ facilitators

**Goal:** Move from 7% highly engaged facilitators to 25%+ within 12 months by enabling peer-to-peer value creation.

## Part 1: Community Architecture in LearnWorlds

### Overview: The SMART Connect Community Structure

Based on research showing facilitators want:
- Discussion boards (78%)
- News & updates (70%)
- Portal for questions (61%)
- Special interest groups (52%)

### LearnWorlds Forum Structure

**Main Community Spaces:**

### Why This Structure?

**Based on Research:**
- **Yarning Circle** = Main discussion board (78% want this)
- **News & Updates** = 70% want this, clear separate space
- **Communities of Practice** = 52% want special interest groups, enables cultural safety
- **Connection Hub** = Addresses #2 support need (47% want co-facilitation)
- **Resources** = Peer-created value (Reddit/Notion model)
- **Facilitator Spotlight** = 43% want this, builds authenticity

**Avoiding Pitfalls:**
- No gamification/leaderboards (only 9% want)
- No mandatory live sessions (time constraints)
- Private Mob space (cultural safety)
- Optional participation in all spaces

## Part 2: Engagement Strategy - First Principles

### The Engagement Equation

**From Research:**
Engagement = Value Created / Time Required

**For Time-Poor Facilitators:**
- Must create value in <5 minutes OR be consumable while multitasking (podcast)
- Asynchronous-first
- Peer-to-peer value creation (not just staff-led)

### Engagement Tactics from World-Class Communities

#### 1. **Reddit Model: Peer Value Creation**

**Apply to SMART:**
- Facilitators answer each other's questions (not just staff)
- "Helpful" reactions on posts (simple recognition without badges)
- Weekly "Best of" digest sent via email (drives people back to see full discussions)

**Implementation:**
- Seed questions that facilitators can answer (not just staff can answer)
- Highlight peer-to-peer exchanges in News & Updates
- Train facilitator "champions" to be active responders

#### 2. **Discord Model: Always-On Async**

**Apply to SMART:**
- Different channels for different needs (not one big forum)
- Clear purpose for each space
- Low barrier to post (no need for perfect posts)

**Implementation:**
- Use LearnWorlds sub-forums as "channels"
- Encourage quick posts, casual tone
- Staff model casual engagement (not just announcements)

#### 3. **Peloton Model: Instructor-Led Community**

**Apply to SMART:**
- Featured facilitators share their approaches (not just anonymous crowd)
- "Facilitator of the Week" spotlight
- Live moments (optional) + async digest for those who can't attend

**Implementation:**
- Weekly facilitator spotlight (rotating demographics)
- Optional monthly live "Yarning Circle" (recorded)
- Async summary for time-poor facilitators

#### 4. **Communities of Practice Model: Structured Peer Learning**

**Apply to SMART:**
- Regular touchpoints (e.g., Monthly CoP threads)
- Shared challenges and solutions
- Resource building together

**Implementation:**
- Monthly topic thread in each CoP space
- Facilitator-led discussions (not staff-led)
- Document shared learnings as resources

#### 5. **Notion/Figma Model: User-Generated Templates**

**Apply to SMART:**
- Facilitators share session plans, scripts, tools
- Templates become currency of value
- Easy to contribute, easy to use

**Implementation:**
- "Resource Drop" section where facilitators upload templates
- Simple format: PDF, Word doc, or even photos of handwritten notes
- Staff curates monthly "Top 5 Resources" based on downloads/reactions

## Part 5: Cultural Safety Framework

### Mob Yarning Space Protocols

**Design Principles (Co-Designed with Aboriginal Facilitators):**

1. **Private & Invite-Only**
   - Not visible to non-members
   - Aboriginal facilitators can nominate others
   - Staff verify facilitator status but don't gatekeep identity
   - Clear invitation message explaining purpose

2. **Ground Rules (Developed with Aboriginal Leadership):**
   - Respect for Country and cultural protocols
   - What's shared in Mob space stays in Mob space (unless explicit consent to share wider)
   - No "big-noting" - recognition done collectively
   - Yarning approach - conversational, not formal
   - Right to edit or remove contributions anytime (reversible consent)

3. **Moderation**
   - At least one Aboriginal facilitator trained as moderator (paid stipend)
   - Cultural safety incidents escalated to Aboriginal leadership
   - Monthly check-ins with Mob space participants
   - Continuous improvement based on feedback

4. **Content Focus**
   - Cultural adaptation of SMART tools
   - Challenges specific to working in Aboriginal communities
   - Connection with other mob facilitators
   - Celebration of cultural approaches to recovery
   - Resource sharing (culturally adapted tools)

5. **Governance**
   - Advisory group of 3-5 Aboriginal facilitators
   - Quarterly reviews of space effectiveness
   - Decision-making power over space rules and structure
   - Budget allocation for Aboriginal facilitator-led initiatives

### Broader Cultural Safety Practices

**All Community Spaces:**
- Language matters: "Group agreements" not "ground rules"
- No anonymous posting (accountability important)
- Clear reporting mechanisms for inappropriate content
- Moderator training on cultural competency
- Diverse representation in spotlights and featured content
- Accessibility considerations (screen readers, plain language)

## Part 6: Moderation Framework

### Moderation Team Structure

**Community Manager (Staff):**
- Overall community strategy
- Content curation and amplification
- Moderator support and training
- Data analysis and reporting
- 10-15 hours/week

**Community Champions (5-10 Facilitators):**
- Active in specific CoP spaces
- Seed discussions, welcome new members
- First-line response to questions
- Escalate issues to Community Manager
- $50-100/month stipend or recognition

**Cultural Safety Moderator (Aboriginal Facilitator):**
- Moderate Mob Yarning Space
- Advise on cultural safety across platform
- Support Aboriginal facilitator onboarding
- $100-150/month stipend

**Volunteer Moderators (2-3 Facilitators):**
- Support in high-traffic spaces
- Weekend/evening coverage
- Report issues to Community Manager
- Recognition (not paid)

### Moderation Approach

**Light Touch, Human First:**
- Focus on connection and support, not policing
- Assume good intent, educate first
- Private messages for most issues (not public call-outs)
- Escalate serious violations to leadership

**Common Scenarios:**

1. **Off-Topic Post:**
   - Gently redirect: "This is great! Would fit better in [Space]. Can I move it there?"

2. **Question Goes Unanswered:**
   - Tag relevant facilitator or Community Champion
   - Staff answer if time-sensitive or no peer response in 48 hours

3. **Insensitive Language:**
   - Private message: "Hey, just wanted to check in about [post]. Here's why that language might be problematic..."
   - Invite edit or removal
   - If no response in 24 hours, post hidden with explanation

4. **Cultural Safety Concern:**
   - Immediate escalation to Cultural Safety Moderator
   - Post hidden pending review
   - Private conversation with poster
   - Decision by Aboriginal leadership on next steps

5. **Conflict Between Facilitators:**
   - Private mediation by Community Manager
   - Remind of community guidelines
   - Invite both parties to discuss in good faith
   - If unresolved, escalate to leadership

## Part 8: LearnWorlds-Specific Setup

### Technical Implementation in LearnWorlds

**Community Setup:**
1. Enable LearnWorlds Community feature
2. Create main forum categories (per structure above)
3. Set permissions (who can post, who can moderate)
4. Configure email notifications (default: bi-weekly digest)
5. Enable "Watch Thread" functionality
6. Set up moderation queue
7. Create welcome automation (new user triggers welcome message)

**Integration with Courses:**
- Link relevant community discussions from training courses
- "Continue the conversation in community" CTAs
- Discussion prompts built into course completion
- Community introduction as part of training onboarding

**Mobile Optimization:**
- Test forum on mobile (most facilitators access via phone)
- Ensure notifications work on mobile
- Simplify posting process (voice-to-text friendly)
- Image uploads easy (photos of handwritten notes okay)

**Search & Discovery:**
- Tags/categories for easy finding content
- "Popular this week" section
- Search functionality with filters
- Facilitator profile directory (optional participation)

**Email Integration:**
- Bi-weekly digest automated
- Instant notifications for @mentions (optional)
- New post in "watched" threads (optional)
- Direct reply to email posts to thread (if LearnWorlds supports)

### LearnWorlds Limitations & Workarounds

**If LearnWorlds Doesn't Support:**

1. **Private Sub-Forums (for Mob Space):**
   - Workaround: Use external tool (Circle, Slack, Discord) for Mob space
   - Or: Manual invitation to LearnWorlds group with permissions

2. **Robust Analytics:**
   - Workaround: Export data monthly, analyze in spreadsheet/BI tool
   - Track manually: engagement, posts, resources, etc.

3. **Direct Messaging:**
   - Workaround: Email introductions between facilitators
   - Or: Use LearnWorlds messaging if available
   - Or: Create "Connection Request" threads (public intro, private follow-up)

4. **Resource Library with Downloads:**
   - Workaround: Use LearnWorlds file upload in forum posts
   - Or: Google Drive folder linked from community
   - Or: Create "Resources" course that's just downloads

5. **Event Scheduling:**
   - Workaround: Google Calendar embedded in forum
   - Or: Monthly event thread with RSVP in comments
   - Or: Zoom links posted in News & Updates

## Part 10: Co-Design Interview Protocol

### Testing the Approach with 5+ Facilitators

**Purpose:**
- Validate community structure before full launch
- Explore LearnWorlds functionality through facilitator eyes
- Identify barriers and opportunities
- Co-create cultural safety protocols
- Recruit Founding Facilitators and Community Champions

### Participant Selection

**Recruit 5-7 Facilitators Representing Diversity:**
1. **Aboriginal facilitator** (essential for Mob space co-design)
2. **LGBTQIA+ facilitator** (CoP design input)
3. **Family & Friends facilitator** (different content needs)
4. **Justice/organizational facilitator** (organizational perspective)
5. **New facilitator** (<6 months experience, fresh eyes)
6. **Experienced facilitator** (>3 years, leadership potential)
7. **Rural/regional facilitator** (different access needs)

**Recruitment:**
- Email invitation to previous interview participants
- Honorarium: $100 gift card for 90-minute session
- Option for in-person or video call
- Promise: "Your input will directly shape the community"

### Interview Structure (90 Minutes)

**Part 1: Context Setting (10 min)**
- Share research findings summary
- Present proposed community structure
- Explain LearnWorlds platform
- Set expectation: We want your honest feedback

**Part 2: Walkthrough & Exploration (30 min)**

*Show them LearnWorlds demo/mockup and ask:*

1. **First Impressions:**
   - What stands out to you?
   - What feels inviting? What feels overwhelming?
   - Would you know where to start?

2. **Navigation & Discoverability:**
   - Show them the forum structure (Yarning Circle, CoP spaces, etc.)
   - Can you find where you'd ask a question?
   - Where would you look for resources?
   - What's missing? What's confusing?

3. **Mobile Experience:**
   - Open on phone (if in person) or share mobile screenshots
   - Would you use this on your phone?
   - What would make mobile posting easier?

4. **Content Formats:**
   - Show examples: discussion thread, resource upload, facilitator spotlight
   - What format would you most likely engage with?
   - What format would you contribute?
   - What's too much effort?

**Part 3: Deep Dive - Feature Testing (20 min)**

*Walk through specific features:*

1. **Discussion Threads:**
   - Show example thread with posts/replies
   - Would you feel comfortable posting here?
   - What would make you more likely to reply to others?
   - How do you feel about notifications? (too much? too little?)

2. **Communities of Practice:**
   - Show CoP space structure (e.g., LGBTQIA+, Mob, Family & Friends)
   - Would you participate in [relevant CoP]?
   - What discussions would be valuable there?
   - How do we make it feel safe?

3. **Resource Sharing:**
   - Show resource library/upload functionality
   - Would you upload a resource? What kind?
   - What would make it easier to share?
   - Would you download others' resources?

4. **Connection Features:**
   - Show "Looking for Co-Facilitator" thread example
   - Would you use this?
   - What other ways could facilitators connect?
   - Direct messaging: useful or overwhelming?

**Part 4: Cultural Safety Deep Dive (20 min)**

*Especially with Aboriginal facilitator, but all participants:*

1. **Mob Yarning Space (Private):**
   - Show proposed structure
   - What protocols are needed?
   - How should invitations work?
   - What would make you feel safe here?
   - Who should moderate?
   - What governance is needed?

2. **Broader Community Safety:**
   - What could go wrong in public spaces?
   - How should we handle conflicts?
   - What language/behaviors are not okay?
   - How do we honor diverse cultural protocols around recognition?

3. **Moderator Role:**
   - What should moderators do/not do?
   - Would you consider being a Community Champion? Why/why not?
   - What support would moderators need?

**Part 5: Content Strategy Feedback (10 min)**

*Show proposed content calendar:*

1. **Weekly Rhythm:**
   - Monday: Inspiration, Wednesday: Peer Support, Friday: Resources
   - Too much? Too little? Right balance?
   - What content would bring you back weekly?

2. **Email Digests:**

## Part 12: Pivot Points & Contingencies

### If Engagement is Low (<50 facilitators in Month 1)

**Diagnosis:**
- Conduct 5 quick interviews with non-users: Why not engaging?
- Review analytics: Are people visiting but not posting? Or not visiting at all?
- Check email open rates: Are digests being read?

**Pivots to Try:**
1. Increase direct outreach (personal invitations)
2. Simplify structure (fewer spaces, clearer purpose)
3. Run contest/challenge with incentive
4. Host live event to drive traffic
5. Shift content (maybe less staff-led, more peer prompts)

### If Engagement is Too Staff-Dependent (Months 3-6)

**Diagnosis:**
- Are facilitators only responding to staff, not each other?
- Are there "dead threads" where no one replies?
- Are Community Champions active enough?

**Pivots to Try:**
1. Train Community Champions to seed more discussions
2. Staff deliberately step back (slow response time to encourage peer response)
3. Highlight peer exchanges more prominently
4. Create "Peer Power" campaign celebrating facilitator-to-facilitator support
5. Restructure incentives (recognize peer helpers)

### If Cultural Safety Issues Arise

**Response Protocol:**
1. Immediate: Hide post, notify poster privately
2. Within 24 hours: Cultural Safety Moderator reviews
3. Within 48 hours: Decision on resolution (education, apology, removal)
4. Within 1 week: Community update (what happened, how addressed, what we learned)
5. Ongoing: Review protocols, adjust as needed

**Escalation:**
- Serious incidents go to Aboriginal advisory group
- Pattern of issues triggers governance review
- Community-wide discussion if needed

### If LearnWorlds Limitations Are Blocking Engagement

**Options:**
1. Integrate external tools (Circle, Slack for specific spaces)
2. Request features from LearnWorlds (they may add if multiple customers ask)
3. Workarounds (Google Docs for collaborative resources, etc.)
4. Long-term: Consider platform migration if fundamentally incompatible (but avoid this if possible)

## Conclusion: The Path Forward

This plan is a living document. It's grounded in research but will evolve based on what facilitators actually do (not what we think they'll do).

**Core Principles to Remember:**
1. **Facilitators are time-poor volunteers** - respect their time
2. **Peer-to-peer value creation** is the goal (not staff-led programs)
3. **Cultural safety is non-negotiable** - especially for Aboriginal facilitators
4. **Small tests, fast learning** - iterate based on behavior
5. **Community-led > platform-led** - amplify facilitator voices

By end of Year 1, facilitators should say:
- "I know I'm not alone - I can ask for help here"
- "I've learned practical things from other facilitators"
- "I feel connected to peers who get what I do"
- "This community makes me a better facilitator"

If we achieve that, we've succeeded.

Now let's test this with facilitators and make it real.
