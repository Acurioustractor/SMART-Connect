Summary

### Background and Current Support

- The facilitator currently provides training for new SMART Recovery facilitators and occasional support packages for organizations.
- Current support needs include:
    - Technical assistance with Zoom backend and facilitator portal
    - Access to debriefing or reflection opportunities with other facilitators
    - Clear communication about policy changes (e.g., recent updates on verbal consent procedures)
- As an experienced facilitator, they generally require minimal support, but recognize newer facilitators likely need more.

### Current Online Tools

- Facilitator portal - contains resources but navigation and functionality not always clear
- Facebook group - seen as underutilized with limited engagement
- Google Docs/Slides - used for personal meeting resources and presentation materials
- Email - primary communication channel for updates from SMART

### Potential Platform Features

- Direct messaging with other facilitators for coverage requests and collaboration
- Webinars for skills development and demonstration of best practices
    - The facilitator expressed strong interest in participating in webinars
    - Emphasized importance of recording sessions for asynchronous access
- Resource library in a centralized location
- Staff/researcher Q&A sessions
- Topic-based spaces alongside a main community area
- Regular updates and communications about facilitator support sessions
- Tagging system for easy navigation of content

### Platform Design Considerations

- Preference for "group agreements" rather than "ground rules" language
- Mixed feelings about AI moderation (some hesitation but understanding of resource constraints)
- Support for human moderation, especially during initial platform growth
- Lukewarm response to gamification and badges (concerns about authenticity)
- Strong preference for non-anonymous participation to maintain accountability
- Desire for a platform that encourages authentic engagement among facilitators
- Need for clear communication about scheduled support sessions

### Concerns About Participant Communities

- Expressed significant reservations about creating SMART-owned platforms for participants
- Concerns that this would conflict with SMART's philosophy of self-management
- Worries about blurring boundaries between facilitation and sponsorship/accountability
- Questions about risk management if participants share concerning content

## 🌱 1. Essential 10-minute set (for quick calls or online chat)

1. **“When do you actually need support as a facilitator?”**
    
    *(Prompt them to describe the last time they got stuck or needed help.)*
    
2. **“What’s the first thing you do when that happens?”**
    
    *(Who or what do you turn to?)*
    
3. **“What would make getting that help faster or easier?”**
4. **“If you had a space just for facilitators — what would make you open it twice a week?”**
5. **“What kind of things would you like to *see* from other facilitators?”**
    
    *(Stories, tools, short videos, resource swaps, etc.)*
    
6. **“What would make you feel proud to post or contribute?”**
7. **“What are the barriers that stop you from sharing or asking for help?”**
8. **“How do you like to learn new facilitation techniques?”**
    
    *(Live sessions, quick clips, PDFs, peer chat, etc.)*
    
9. **“What needs to be off-limits or handled carefully to keep everyone safe?”**
10. **“What would make this feel culturally safe and welcoming for you and your community?”**

## 🔍 2. Deep-dive (30–40-minute co-design conversation)

Use this when you’re running a proper discovery interview or small group.

### A. Warm-up

- “Tell me a bit about your SMART group — who attends, what’s working well?”
- “One word for how facilitation feels this week.”

### B. Walk through the *last time you needed help*

- “What triggered it?”
- “What did you try first?”
- “How long did it take to get what you needed?”
- “What was frustrating or surprising?”

### C. Explore the current toolkit

- “What tools, websites, chats, or documents do you use now?”
- “Which of these actually help — and which are a pain?”
- “Show me your current workaround for finding resources.”

### D. Test value of proposed features

Read each feature and ask “Would you use this *twice a week*?” and “Why / why not?”

- DMs with other facilitators
- 20-minute live webinar per month
- Micro-lessons (5–8-min refreshers)
- Resource Drop (one-page tools)
- Staff AMA or researcher Q&A
- Topic spaces (youth, justice, LGBTQIA+, Mob)
- Recognition (badges, shout-outs, opportunities)

### E. Cultural safety and governance

- “What ground rules would make you feel safe sharing here?”
- “How should moderators behave?”
- “If something goes wrong, what’s the right way to handle it?”

### F. Future vision

- “If this platform disappeared tomorrow, what would you miss?”
- “If it works perfectly, what changes in your week?”

### G. Close

- “Would you join the pilot as a Founding Facilitator?”
- “Who else should we talk to?”
- Confirm consent: *“You can edit or withdraw anything you share later.”*
