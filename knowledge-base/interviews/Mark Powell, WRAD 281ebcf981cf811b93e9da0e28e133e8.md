Summary

### Meeting Overview

- Research interview conducted by @Benjamin Knight with Mark, a SMART Recovery facilitator
- Part of a series of 4 interviews exploring requirements for an online community platform for SMART facilitators
- Focus on understanding current practices, pain points, and desired features for facilitator support

### Mark's Facilitation Background

- Delivers SMART Recovery Family and Friends program (not the addiction program)
- Has been facilitating for several years
- Runs groups in an informal format: check-ins, new member introductions, then addressing issues with appropriate tools
- Most common topics: communication, boundaries, and self-care
- Uses group time flexibly rather than following strict 8-session modules
- Directs people wanting formal course structure to other facilitators who deliver week-by-week modules
- Works in the field professionally but runs groups after hours as a volunteer

### Current Tools and Resources Used

- SMART Facilitators Guide and five-step method
- Most commonly used tools: PIUS tool (limitations), CERF (being specific understanding)
- ABC tool occasionally
- Problem-solving approaches
- Motivational interviewing language concepts
- Shows participants where to find tools on SMART website during sessions
- Stays current through podcasts rather than formal professional development
- Listens to American SMART Recovery Podcast regularly
- Hasn't been able to attend facilitator workshops recently due to time constraints

### Platform Feature Feedback

**Direct Messaging Between Facilitators**

- Has never done this before, uncertain if they would use it
- Would like to be more part of a community of practice
- Major barrier: time constraints during work hours
- All facilitation happens after hours as volunteer work

**Live Webinars vs Recorded Content**

- Admits to "wishful thinking" - saves webinar recordings but rarely watches them
- **Podcasts are preferred format** due to lifestyle (listens while exercising in the morning)
- Suggested creating Australian SMART podcast featuring tool discussions and facilitator case studies

**Micro-Courses and Quiz Refreshers**

- Positive response - sees value in this
- Acknowledges risk of getting into comfort zone after facilitating for years
- Challenge: making it easy for volunteers with limited time

**Regular Resource Updates and Prompts**

- Very positive - doesn't need to be new content, could be reminders about existing tools
- Example: Conversation reminded them about mobile app they used to recommend but forgot about

**Ask Me Anything with Staff/Board**

- Lukewarm response
- Could work as alternative to in-person annual gatherings
- In-person events (like awards night in Sydney) are ideal but expensive and difficult for volunteers

**Gamification and Recognition Badges**

- Neutral stance - acknowledges it works for some people
- Compared to streak-keeping in apps
- Uncertain about impact in this context

**Facilitator Profiles**

- Positive - happy to share profile information (LinkedIn, background)
- Sees value in humanizing facilitators
- Important to make profiles optional due to privacy concerns for lived-experience volunteers

**Topic-Specific Spaces**

- **Strongly positive** - "definitely think that's a good idea"
- Specifically interested in Family and Friends facilitator space
- Currently runs unmoderated WhatsApp group for participants (global reach: Ireland, England, America)

**Moderation and Community Guidelines**

- Strongly supports moderation
- Already discusses guidelines at start of meetings (no jargon, no disparaging terminology)
- Mentioned awareness of existing SMART Australia Facebook group but doesn't use it
- Participates in international groups (Allies in Recovery, American groups) occasionally

### Key Insights and Pain Points

**Time Constraints**

- Biggest barrier across all features
- Work commitments make daytime engagement impossible
- Facilitation is after-hours volunteer work
- Difficult to take time for professional development

**Isolation from Facilitator Community**

- Operates in "own little world"
- Would like to connect with other Family and Friends facilitators but no easy way to do so
- Example: Knows another online Family and Friends facilitator but has no means to readily connect

**Meeting Register is Critical**

- If all SMART online resources disappeared, meeting register would be most missed
- Primary reason for joining SMART was to have platform for people to find groups
- Suggests meeting search could be more developed

**Organizational Visibility Challenges**

- SMART competes with other organizations (Sharq in Victoria, Family Drug Support nationally)
- Most participants come from NSW where SMART started
- Needs more promotion and visibility
- Featuring facilitators could help people find groups that fit them

**Evidence-Based Approach Valued**

- Appreciates that SMART tools are evidence-based
- Contrasts with "tough love" and traditional AA/Al-Anon approaches
- Important distinguishing factor

**Participant Support Channel**

- Would love participant channel but capacity is major concern
- Currently encourages participants to find local counsellors for individual support
- Open to answering brief questions
- Already does this informally via WhatsApp group

**Content Creation Opportunities**

- Open to participating in webinars, interviews, or creating posts
- Sees value in promoting facilitators
- Helps with personal visibility in the field

### Suggestions for Australian SMART

- Develop Australian SMART podcast featuring tool experts and facilitator case studies
- Create spaces for participants to realize others share similar experiences
- Improve meeting search functionality
- Consider annual in-person gatherings (funding permitting)
- Feature facilitators to humanize the service

## 🌱 1. Essential 10-minute set (for quick calls or online chat)

1. **“When do you actually need support as a facilitator?”**
    
    *(Prompt them to describe the last time they got stuck or needed help.)*
    
2. **“What’s the first thing you do when that happens?”**
    
    *(Who or what do you turn to?)*
    
3. **“What would make getting that help faster or easier?”**
4. **“If you had a space just for facilitators — what would make you open it twice a week?”**
5. **“What kind of things would you like to *see* from other facilitators?”**
    
    *(Stories, tools, short videos, resource swaps, etc.)*
    
6. **“What would make you feel proud to post or contribute?”**
7. **“What are the barriers that stop you from sharing or asking for help?”**
8. **“How do you like to learn new facilitation techniques?”**
    
    *(Live sessions, quick clips, PDFs, peer chat, etc.)*
    
9. **“What needs to be off-limits or handled carefully to keep everyone safe?”**
10. **“What would make this feel culturally safe and welcoming for you and your community?”**

## 🔍 2. Deep-dive (30–40-minute co-design conversation)

Use this when you’re running a proper discovery interview or small group.

### A. Warm-up

- “Tell me a bit about your SMART group — who attends, what’s working well?”
- “One word for how facilitation feels this week.”

### B. Walk through the *last time you needed help*

- “What triggered it?”
- “What did you try first?”
- “How long did it take to get what you needed?”
- “What was frustrating or surprising?”

### C. Explore the current toolkit

- “What tools, websites, chats, or documents do you use now?”
- “Which of these actually help — and which are a pain?”
- “Show me your current workaround for finding resources.”

### D. Test value of proposed features

Read each feature and ask “Would you use this *twice a week*?” and “Why / why not?”

- DMs with other facilitators
- 20-minute live webinar per month
- Micro-lessons (5–8-min refreshers)
- Resource Drop (one-page tools)
- Staff AMA or researcher Q&A
- Topic spaces (youth, justice, LGBTQIA+, Mob)
- Recognition (badges, shout-outs, opportunities)

### E. Cultural safety and governance

- “What ground rules would make you feel safe sharing here?”
- “How should moderators behave?”
- “If something goes wrong, what’s the right way to handle it?”

### F. Future vision

- “If this platform disappeared tomorrow, what would you miss?”
- “If it works perfectly, what changes in your week?”

### G. Close

- “Would you join the pilot as a Founding Facilitator?”
- “Who else should we talk to?”
- Confirm consent: *“You can edit or withdraw anything you share later.”*
