## Key Themes Across Interviews

### Isolation & Need for Peer Connection
- Facilitators feel they operate in "own little world" (Mark Powell)
- Need to connect with others who "get it"
- Desire for peer-to-peer support, not just staff-led

### Time Constraints
- "I save quite a few [webinar recordings] and never get around to them" (Mark Powell)
- Time-poor volunteers
- Prefer asynchronous engagement
- Podcasts > webinars

### Cultural Safety (Critical Theme)
- **Danielle Caruana:** "If it's culturally safe, that's the main thing for me"
- Need for private Aboriginal facilitator space (Mob Yarning Space)
- Cultural adaptation of SMART tools is skilled work
- Protocols must be embedded from Day 1, not added later

### Gamification & Recognition
- **Ambika:** Concerns about badges/gamification feeling inauthentic
- Survey data: Only 9% want badges/rewards
- Prefer authentic recognition over performative systems

### Facilitator Burnout
- Lack of self-care practices
- Holding space for others without support for themselves
- Need for boundaries and peer support

### Handling Challenging Situations
- Dominant participants
- Participants in crisis
- Balancing individual needs with group needs
- Managing silence in meetings
